### 1. Storage and Accessibility

It is important to store and organise research files efficiently because the project contains many types of data, including literature, survey data, interview transcripts, scripts, reports, and media files. A clear structure reduces the risk of losing files, using outdated versions, or sharing sensitive data incorrectly, especially when several researchers are working on the same project.

### 2. File Storage Methods

I would organise the materials by data type and research stage. For example, literature review files would be stored in a `literature_review` folder, raw survey and interview data in a restricted `data/raw` folder, analysis scripts and outputs in an `analysis` folder, and draft or final reports in a `reports` folder. Consent forms, identifiable survey data, interview transcripts, and photos should have special access controls because they may contain private or ethically sensitive information.
