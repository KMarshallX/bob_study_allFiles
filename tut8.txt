Activity 1 Q1 - Explain why it is important to store and organise your files efficiently. (2-3 sentences)

Organising files efficiently makes it much easier to find said files, especially compared to dumping hundreds of files in a single directory.

Activity 1 Q2 - Explain your methods for storing the different types of data in the scenario. Specify which data types may require special access controls. (2-3 sentences)

I just organised the files into the categories defined by the handout, for example:
- 'Literature Review' should contain journal and conference articles, books, newspaper, articles.
- 'Quantitative Analysis' contains survey data, survey questions, analysis files, survey analysis reports.
- 'Qualitative Analysis' contains several interview transcripts, interview protocols, consent forms, analysis reports, and data visualisations.
- 'Drafts and Reports' contains draft research proposals, conference papers, and final reports.
- 'Additional Materials' contains Information sheets, photos, and other media files.

Some assumptions had to be made, e.g. 'Photo_Interview_Consent_Form.jpg' is both a photo and a consent form, but I decided to keep all photos in the 'Additional Materials' directory.

Special access controls may need to be used for some files in 'Quantitative Analysis' as it can contain confidential personal information in the survey data.

Activity 2 Q1 - Why is it important to have a good naming convention? (2-3 sentences)

A good naming convention allows for much easier identification of files. For example, it should include a relevant name for fairly obvious reasons, and a version number and/or date as you can use it to identify older copies of a file.
