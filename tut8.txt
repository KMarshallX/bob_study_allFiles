Activity 1
Q1 -Efficient file storage and organisation are important because research projects often involve many different types of materials, such as literature, survey data, scripts, interview transcripts, reports, and media files. A clear structure makes it easier for team members to find the correct files, avoid duplication, track changes, and reduce the risk of losing or misusing important research data.
Q2 -Different types of research data should be stored in clearly separated folders, such as Literature_Review, Quantitative_Analysis, Qualitative_Analysis, Drafts_Reports, and Additional_Materials. Sensitive files, such as interview transcripts, consent forms, survey responses, and identifiable participant information, should have restricted access and should not be stored in a public GitHub repository. These files should be kept in secure university-approved storage with controlled permissions.
